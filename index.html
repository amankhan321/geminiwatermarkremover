How it works

Not a blur. An exact undo.

Gemini stamps its sparkle with plain alpha blending using a fixed, known mask. Knowing the mask means the blend can be run backwards — recovering the pixels underneath instead of guessing them.

01 — LOCATE

Find the sparkle

Gemini's known placements are checked first. If the logo isn't there, a size-and-inset search sweeps the whole corner, so new layouts, resized and re-saved copies are found too.

02 — VERIFY

Prove it before touching

A candidate only counts if un-blending makes the shape vanish. Images without the logo are left byte-for-byte untouched — no false "fixes".

03 — RESTORE

Reverse the blend

Every pixel under the mask is solved for its original value. The logo's real strength is fitted from the image, and the thin outline that compression leaves is repaired.

watermarked = α·255 + (1 − α)·original original = (watermarked − α·255) / (1 − α)

α is the calibrated per-pixel opacity of the sparkle. Outside the logo, nothing is touched. Choose PNG output for a truly lossless round-trip.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. Decoding, detection, removal and ZIP packaging all happen inside your browser tab. You can load the page, go offline, and it keeps working.

How many images can I do at once?

There's no limit. Images are processed in parallel across your CPU cores; very large batches are only bound by your device's memory.

Why was an image marked "No logo found"?

Either it has no visible sparkle, or the corner was heavily edited, cropped or recompressed. Use "Force remove on all skipped" in the toolbar, or set Auto-remove to Always before adding images.

Will quality drop?

Only the pixels under the logo change. Pick PNG output for a lossless file; JPG/WebP output re-encodes at the quality you choose (95 by default).

Does this remove SynthID?

No. Sparkless only removes the visible sparkle. Google's invisible SynthID watermark and any C2PA metadata are not touched.

Which images work best?

Original downloads straight from Gemini, AI Studio or Nano Banana. Screenshots and social-media re-uploads still work most of the time, with slightly softer results.
